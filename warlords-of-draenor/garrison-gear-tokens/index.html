Non-appearance tokens have been added to show they may be encountered, however useless they may be.

If you’re looking to do this with as few characters as possible, your main concern are the weapon tokens. The following setup gives you the chance to get all possible weapon appearances:

  • Priest (Spires, Scepters)
  • Rogue (Daggers, Axes, Warmaces)
  • Hunter (Bows, Polearms)
  • Any Plate class (Greataxes, Axes) - Paladins can also get Warmaces

Mission Spawning Requirements

Each type of mission, and sometimes each mission itself, has different requirements to be spawned. See the table below:

Token TypeFollower amount and levelPlayer item level
Tormented1 x 31-39*Any
Munificent 44 or lower**
Turbulent3 x 630+44 or lower**
Grandiose3 x 645+44 or lower**

*See the first table on the page, in which the Tormented items have a number next to them. In order for their mission to spawn, you must have a follower of that exact level. It does not matter if the follower is active or inactive.
**This terribly low item level can be achieved by either leveling a new character and keeping it below the threshold, or deleting gear on a high-level character. Note that keeping gear in bags will not work, as that is also included in the calculation.

In addition, it seems more likely that missions spawn the more followers you have. It is unknown at this time whether more followers of a specific level results in more chances of missions of that specific level spawning.

Turbulent Gear Tokens

The following four tokens can only be acquired at random from the https://www.wowhead.com/item=118924/cache-of-arms#contains , a reward from the Garrison Inn quest https://www.wowhead.com/quest=37167/and-no-maces :

  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=114073/turbulent-hood
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=114072/turbulent-gauntlets
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=114074/turbulent-leggings
  • https://www.wowhead.com/item=114079/turbulent-cloak

It is possible the quest may not be offered by the NPC at all Inn levels, which would explain why some characters will have a different quest available. Also note the quest always rewards 2 tokens - one cloak, and one of the others. Furthermore, the quest is not repeatable. You’ll need to throw an army of alts at this, especially if you want the cloak sources.

Formidable Tokens & Greater Bounty Spoils

Formidable tokens cannot be acquired through Garrison missions. They can only be obtained from greater-bounty-spoils . This means that, upon opening this bag, you have a chance at receiving either a Grandiose or a Formidable token. If you’re targeting a specific token, this means you have a 1/26 chance of actually getting it, once per week, per character.

Here’s a breakdown of how to get greater-bounty-spoils :

  • Every day, you have a visitor in your Tier 3 Garrison. Sometimes a vendor, sometimes a questgiver.
    • Alliance: you’re looking for https://www.wowhead.com/npc=89763/muradin-bronzebeard
    • Horde: you’re looking for https://www.wowhead.com/npc=89753/high-overlord-saurfang
    • If he’s not up in your Garrison, you can totally visit a friend’s Garrison if they have him.
  • When spawned, you will be offered a quest to kill the final boss of a WOD raid wing.
    • Once you complete one of these quests, you’re locked from accepting any more until the weekly reset.
    • There are 12 quests in total, on a fixed rotation.
    • Five of them, sending you to Hellfire Citadel, award Apexis Crystals instead of Greater Bounty Spoils
  • Pick up the quest, go into the specified LFR wing via https://www.wowhead.com/npc=94870/seer-kazal and complete the quest.
  • Once completed, that character cannot acquire Greater Bounty Spoils until the weekly reset.

From personal testing, Grandiose tokens seem far more common than Formidable tokens.

Notes

  • Garrison missions spawn randomly. Some day you may find a mission for a token, other days not.
  • You can purchase https://www.wowhead.com/item=128307/draenic-weaponry and https://www.wowhead.com/item=128308/draenic-armor-set from the <Local Supplies> vendor near your Mission Table to quickly give a level 40 follower a high item level. The vendor only appears in Level 3 Garrisons.
  • Alternatively, https://www.wowhead.com/item=128314/frozen-arms-of-a-hero can be used (available on the AH).
  • Sometimes you may see a Grandiose mission with only two 645+ followers. It’s unclear what triggers it.
