## Collecting

- [ALL THE THINGS](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/all-the-things) is a massive effort to track every collectible
  item in the game. You almost certainly want this, though it does tend to be quite memory hungry.

### Auctions

- [Auctionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/auctionator) and [Collectionator](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/collectionator)
  combine to make it simple to buy missing things from the auction house.

### Maps

- [HandyNotes](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/handynotes) displays icons and notes on your map. There are a
  [LOT of plugins](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/search?search=handynotes) available, you probably want a full set of
  Kemayo's or Zarillion's per-expansion plugins to start with.
- [TomTom](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tomtom) shows a handy arrow pointing you towards waypoints. Many other addons integrate with this.

### Pet Battles

- [Rematch](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rematch) blurb here.
- [FixRematch](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/fixrematch) so that Rematch actually works.
- [Pet Battle Scripts](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/pet-battle-scripts) to mostly automate repetitive battles.

### Quests

- [ExtraQuestButton](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/extraquestbutton) adds a button that will show the quest item for the
  closest quest with a usable item.

### Rare Spawns

- [NPC Time](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/npctime) shows how long an NPC has been alive in tooltips. Very useful for
  knowing how old a world shard is for rares with specific spawn windows. **NOTE:** may be killed by 12.1.7 changes :(
- [RareScanner](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/rarescanner) and [SilverDragon](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/silver-dragon)
  will both show alerts when rares/treasures spawn.

### Transmog

- [Can I Mog It?](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/can-i-mog-it) shows useful bag overlays and tooltips for collected
  status.
- [Transmog Loot Helper](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/transmog-loot-helper) is a more recent take on the same idea.
